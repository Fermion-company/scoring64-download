Every feature free for 2 tests a month

Each computer can grade 2 tests a month with every feature and no limit on students. Sheet design, written-answer grading, gradebooks and returned PDFs are all included. Using the bubble sheet reader

A test counts once, the first time you grade it, and the free tests reset on the 1st of each month, Japan time (JST). Adding answer sheets, regrading and exporting the same test do not use another. A paid license is needed from the third new test in a month.

When the free tests run out, only grading a new test stops. You can still continue, correct and export tests you have started, design sheets, and view, back up and restore your data.

Pricing outside Japan

We are setting prices for schools and tutoring centers outside Japan. For now, email contact@fermion.company with your school or center name, your country and how many computers you plan to use, and we will reply with a quote and how to pay. For several sites, we quote per site.

How a license key is issued

  1. Open Scoring64 on the computer you will use and click the counter at the top right (2 free tests left this month, for example) or License. In Free plan and license, click the This computer's code field to select all of it, and copy it (Ctrl + C on Windows, Command + C on a Mac). If you have not installed the app yet, start with Download and install.
  2. Email the code to contact@fermion.company with your school or center name. For a renewal, include your License ID as well.
  3. When the key for that computer arrives by email, paste all of it into the key field in Free plan and license on the same computer and click Register. Your exams and grading carry on where you left them.

You do not need all five computers at the start. Send the code of the first one; we issue a key for each further computer from its own code, up to five in one classroom. You never need to send answer sheets, grades or class lists.
